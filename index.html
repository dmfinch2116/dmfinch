Important distinction

Live Storm Prediction Center reports are preliminary sightings/reports, not a list of confirmed tornadoes. They generally do not include an official EF rating or a surveyed start/end time. Ratings, path duration and track details are added after National Weather Service damage surveys; a missing rating does not mean EF0.

Data & safety

Reports: NOAA/NWS Storm Prediction Center. This app is for situational awareness, not life-safety alerts. Follow official warnings and local emergency instructions.
